I'm a photographer and visual designer based in Bucharest, Romania, specializing in architecture, real estate, hospitality, and graphic design. I work with developers, architects, restaurants, and hotels to create imagery that communicates their vision and drives results.

Every project starts with understanding the space, the light, and the story behind it, whether it's an aerial view of a new residential complex, the atmosphere of a restaurant at golden hour, or a dish photographed to make you reach for the menu. The result is always visuals that are technically sharp and emotionally resonant.
